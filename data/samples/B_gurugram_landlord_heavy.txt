RENT AGREEMENT (LEAVE AND LICENCE)

This agreement is executed at Gurugram on 1st November 2026 between Mr. Vikram Malhotra, owner of the property, mobile 98110 45678 (the "Licensor") and Mr. Arjun Mehta, Aadhaar 4521 7788 9012, email arjun.mehta@example.com (the "Licensee").

1. Premises: Flat No. B-1104, Skyline Towers, Sector 54, Gurugram, fully furnished 3BHK, to be used for residential purposes.

2. Term: The licence is granted for 11 months starting 1st November 2026.

3. Rent: The Licensee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 45,000 (Rupees Forty Five Thousand only) in advance on or before the 1st of every month.

4. Security Deposit: The Licensee shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 4,50,000 (Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand only). The deposit shall be refunded only after the Licensor finds a new tenant for the premises. If the Licensee leaves before the end of the term for any reason, the entire security deposit shall stand forfeited.

5. Lock-in: The Licensee shall not terminate this agreement for the first 11 months (lock-in period). If the Licensee vacates during the lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay rent for the balance of the lock-in period.

6. Termination: The Licensee must give 3 (three) months' written notice to vacate. The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time by giving 7 days' notice.

7. Rent Increase: The rent shall be increased by 15% every year or as decided by the Licensor at the time of renewal.

8. Late Payment: If the rent is not paid by the due date, a late fee of Rs. 1,000 per day of delay shall be payable by the Licensee.

9. Repairs: All repairs of whatever nature, including structural repairs, seepage, and repair or replacement of furniture and appliances, shall be done by the Licensee at his own cost.

10. Access: The Licensor or his agents may enter the premises at any time without notice for inspection or to show the flat to prospective tenants or buyers.

11. Painting: At the time of vacating, one month's rent shall be deducted from the security deposit towards painting and cleaning, irrespective of the condition of the premises.

12. Use: No guests shall stay overnight in the premises without the prior written permission of the Licensor. Pets are not allowed.

13. Maintenance Charges: Society maintenance charges, as decided by the Licensor from time to time, shall be paid by the Licensee in addition to the rent.

14. Stamp Duty: Stamp duty and registration charges shall be borne entirely by the Licensee.

15. Disputes: Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Licensor, and the seat of arbitration shall be New Delhi.
