LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 1st November 2026 between Mr. Prakash Rao, residing at 14, 3rd Cross, Jayanagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter "the Licensor"), phone 98450 11223, and Ms. Neha Kulkarni, PAN ABCPK1234D, email neha.k@example.com (hereinafter "the Licensee").

1. Premises: The Licensor grants the Licensee a licence to use Flat No. 302, Sunrise Residency, HSR Layout, Bengaluru, a 2BHK semi-furnished flat, for residential purposes only.

2. Period: This licence is for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st November 2026 and ending on 30th September 2027.

3. Licence Fee: The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 28,000 (Rupees Twenty Eight Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.

4. Security Deposit: The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,68,000 (Rupees One Lakh Sixty Eight Thousand only). The deposit shall be refunded within 15 days of the Licensee vacating the premises, after deducting only unpaid dues and the actual cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear, supported by bills.

5. Lock-in Period: Both parties agree to a lock-in period of 6 (six) months. If either party terminates during the lock-in period, that party shall pay one month's licence fee to the other.

6. Termination and Notice: After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by giving 1 (one) month's written notice to the other.

7. Escalation: On renewal, the licence fee shall be increased by 5% over the last paid licence fee.

8. Maintenance and Repairs: Society maintenance charges are included in the licence fee. Minor repairs up to Rs. 1,000 per instance (bulbs, taps, fuses) shall be borne by the Licensee. Structural repairs, plumbing, seepage and repairs of appliances provided by the Licensor shall be borne by the Licensor.

9. Utilities: The Licensee shall pay electricity and piped gas bills as per actual meter readings.

10. Inspection: The Licensor may inspect the premises at a mutually convenient time after giving at least 24 hours' prior notice to the Licensee.

11. Painting and Cleaning: At the time of vacating, the actual cost of painting, if required due to damage beyond normal wear and tear, shall be deducted from the deposit, supported by bills.

12. Use: The Licensee shall not sublet the premises. Family members and guests of the Licensee are permitted.

13. Stamp Duty and Registration: Stamp duty and registration charges shall be shared equally by the Licensor and the Licensee.

14. Disputes: Any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru.
